Every autumn a wave of "everything that changes on 1 January" articles appears in the Dutch media, and every autumn a good share of the numbers in them turn out to be projections that were quietly revised before the year began. For a reader who cannot easily cross-check the Dutch originals, that is a real problem: you make decisions on figures that were never confirmed.

So this roundup uses three buckets — **confirmed**, **proposed but not yet law**, and **not yet published** — with a source on every item, all verified on 20 August 2026. Where a 2027 figure does not exist yet, you get the mechanism and the official page to watch rather than a number.

> **Short answer:** Confirmed for 1 January 2027: the expat ruling drops from a maximum of 30% to **27%** (with pre-2024 entrants protected), and the **minimum youth wage rises** for 16-to-20-year-olds. Confirmed as *unchanged*: the **AOW age stays at 67** in 2027. Only proposed: raising the mandatory health insurance **eigen risico to €455**, a bill still in parliament at the time of verification. Not yet published as of 20 August 2026: 2027 health insurance premiums, the January minimum wage amounts, child benefit amounts, and the 2027 box 3 percentages — box 3 keeps its current structure because its replacement is aimed at 1 January **2028**.

## Confirmed: the expat ruling drops to 27%

Rijksoverheid states that the expat ruling's tax-free allowance stays at a maximum of 30% in 2025 and 2026 and drops to a maximum of 27% from 2027, with a higher salary norm from 2027 for later entrants. The published transition table protects anyone who started using the ruling on or before 31 December 2023: they keep 30% and the current salary norm ([rijksoverheid.nl — expatregeling](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/belastingvoordeel-buitenlandse-werknemers)).

For most readers of this site this is the biggest item on the list, because it lands directly in monthly take-home pay. Which row of the transition table you sit in decides whether it affects you at all — the full breakdown, including the 2026 salary norms of €48,013 and €36,497, is in [the 30% ruling in 2027](/blog/30-percent-ruling-2027-changes-expats/).

## Confirmed: higher minimum youth wage

From 1 January 2027 the minimum youth wage rises for 16 to 20 year olds. Per rijksoverheid's announcement of 17 April 2026, each age band moves to a higher percentage of the adult minimum wage: 20 years to 87.5% (from 80%), 19 to 75% (from 60%), 18 to 62.5% (from 50%), 17 to 50% (from 39.5%), and 16 to 40% (from 34.5%). The youth wage for students in the work-based vocational pathway (*bbl*) is aligned with the regular minimum youth wage ([hoger minimumjeugdloon vanaf 2027](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/04/17/hoger-minimumjeugdloon-vanaf-2027)).

If you have teenagers with part-time jobs, or you employ young staff, this is a real change rather than an indexation tweak — the jump for 19-year-olds is fifteen percentage points.

## Confirmed unchanged: the AOW age and box 3's structure

The state pension age stays at **67** in 2027 — rijksoverheid lists 67 for 2025 through 2027 and 67 years and 3 months for 2028 through 2031, and the age must be fixed five years ahead and cannot change once set ([AOW-leeftijd 2025-2031](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/algemene-ouderdomswet-aow/aow-leeftijd)). For expats the build-up rule matters more than the age: AOW accrues per year of residence or insured employment, so years abroad leave a gap — see the [Dutch pension system guide](/guides/finance/dutch-pension-system-expats-2026/) and [claiming a Dutch pension abroad](/blog/claiming-dutch-pension-abroad-2026/).

Box 3 also keeps its current shape. Its replacement, the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3, was passed by the Tweede Kamer on 12 February 2026 and adopted by the Eerste Kamer on 7 July 2026, with intended commencement on 1 January **2028** ([rijksoverheid timeline](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/plannen-werkelijk-rendement-box-3/tijdlijn-werkelijk-rendement-box-3); [Eerste Kamer dossier 36.748](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)). So 2027 is another year of assumed-return percentages — see [box 3 in 2027](/blog/box-3-tax-netherlands-2027-expats/).

## Proposed, not settled: the eigen risico

This is the item most likely to be reported as fact before it is one. A bill proposes raising the mandatory health insurance deductible to **€455** in 2027 — indexation plus an additional €60 — with a plenary debate scheduled for 21 September 2026 ([Tweede Kamer, bill on the verplicht eigen risico per 2027](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/detail?id=2026Z09664&did=2026D21810)). At the verification date the rijksoverheid Q&A page listed only the 2026 amount of €385 and said nothing about 2027 ([eigen risico zorgverzekering](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/zorgverzekering/eigen-risico-zorgverzekering)).

So: a proposal in parliamentary process, subject to approval, with a figure that could change during passage. Budget for uncertainty on this line rather than a specific amount. How the deductible works, and which care it applies to, is in [eigen risico explained](/blog/eigen-risico-dutch-deductible-explained-2026/).

## Not yet published: 2027 health insurance premiums

Insurers publish their premiums for the coming year in the autumn, in practice during November. Any 2027 premium figure circulating in October is a forecast, and the spread between forecasts is usually wider than the differences you are choosing between.

What is fixed is the calendar: cancel your current policy **before 31 December 2026** if you intend to switch, and have the new one in place **before 1 February 2027**, backdated to 1 January. Insurers must accept you for the basic insurance regardless of health; supplementary cover is where acceptance conditions and waiting periods apply. This is the one piece of Dutch consumer admin where acting in December reliably beats acting in January — step by step in [how to switch Dutch health insurance](/blog/how-to-switch-health-insurance-netherlands-step-by-step-2026/), with the deadline detail in [the January 2027 deadline article](/blog/health-insurance-deadline-netherlands-january-2027/).

## Not yet published: minimum wage and child benefit amounts

**The statutory minimum wage** is indexed twice a year, on 1 January and 1 July, with the January amounts confirmed in the autumn on the government's [minimum wage amounts page](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/minimumloon/bedragen-minimumloon). Adult indexation is separate from the minimum *youth* wage increase above.

**Kinderbijslag** amounts are likewise indexed and published by the SVB, and none were available for 2027 on 20 August 2026 — so this article quotes none. The same annual rhythm applies to zorgtoeslag and huurtoeslag thresholds, where a change in your own income matters more than indexation does. For the system itself: [childcare allowance](/blog/childcare-allowance-kinderopvangtoeslag-expats-2026/), the [childcare cost calculator](/blog/childcare-cost-calculator-netherlands-2026/), [how expats claim Dutch benefits](/guides/finance/how-expats-claim-dutch-benefits-toeslagen-2026/) and [zorgtoeslag for expats](/blog/zorgtoeslag-healthcare-allowance-expats-2026/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is definitely changing on 1 January 2027?

The expat ruling drops to a maximum of 27% (with pre-2024 entrants protected), and the minimum youth wage rises for 16-to-20-year-olds. Both are published on rijksoverheid.nl.

### Is the eigen risico rising to €455?

That is a proposal, with a plenary debate scheduled for 21 September 2026. It was not settled law at the verification date, and the government's Q&A page listed only the 2026 figure of €385.

### What is the AOW age in 2027?

67, unchanged. It rises to 67 years and 3 months from 2028.

### When are the 2027 health insurance premiums published?

In the autumn, in practice during November. The switching deadlines do not change. Box 3, meanwhile, is not replaced in 2027 — that law is aimed at 1 January 2028.

### Why are there no 2027 minimum wage or child benefit figures here?

They were not published on 20 August 2026, so this article links the official pages instead of printing a projection.

## My final thoughts

The honest summary of 1 January 2027 for most internationals here: one significant tax change, one benefit increase for households with teenagers, one contested health insurance proposal, and a long list of figures that simply did not exist in August.

Two narrow things are worth doing this autumn. Work out which row of the expat ruling transition table applies to you, because that is the change with a direct monthly effect. And put the health insurance switching window in your December calendar, because it is the one deadline where acting on time is worth money regardless of what the premiums turn out to be.
